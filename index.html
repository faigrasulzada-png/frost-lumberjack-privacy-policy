Privacy Policy

Frost Lumberjack: Survival — Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Frost Lumberjack: Survival" (the "Game") handles information when you play it.

Summary

The Game does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no internet connection required to play.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details.

Data Stored On Your Device

The Game saves your progress (such as your level, resources, and settings) locally on your own device only, using standard local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never sent to us or any third party
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the Game or clear its app data

Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not use services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, or similar tools.

Permissions

The Game may request standard device permissions (such as vibration for haptic feedback) solely to provide its core functionality. These permissions are not used to collect or transmit any data.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected at all, the Game is safe for players of any age with respect to data privacy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if ads or analytics are added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
